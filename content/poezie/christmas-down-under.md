<div lang="en">

From his time spent down under, an elf had returned<br/>
To tell Santa all of the things he had learned.<br/>
"Their Christmas is odd, for snow there is not,<br/>
Instead lots of sunscreen as the sun is so hot."<br/>
Said Santa: "do not worry, they're not missing out,<br/>
'Tis family and good times what Christmas is all about."

<hr>

Santa tied presents with rope to a cork,<br/>
And said to himself: "I hope this will work".<br/>
He shook really hard with his grand champagne bottle,<br/>
And pointed it south where it flew to full throttle.<br/>
"Fly off to Australia to bring presents in my stead"<br/>
But it missed and instead hit the back of his head.
